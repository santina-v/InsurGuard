POLICE ACCIDENT REPORT

Incident Date: March 15, 2026
Incident Time: 3:00 PM
Location: Chicago, Illinois

Weather Conditions: Clear, sunny skies.

The claimant, Daniel Carter, was driving a 2020 Toyota Camry
when his vehicle struck another vehicle at the intersection
of North Clark Street and West Fullerton Avenue.

The other vehicle was stopped at the intersection.
Based on the scene investigation, the claimant was determined
to be at fault.

Officer Notes:
The claimant stated that he was traveling through the
intersection when the collision occurred. Visible damage
was observed on the vehicles after the collision.
